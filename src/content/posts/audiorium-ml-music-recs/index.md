Audiorium is a product concept built on a music recommendation algorithm that I created using contrastive language audio pretraining, or CLAP. this algorithm can take plain text and images as input to search for music that is most similar to your exact search, rather than the most popular songs. this project was inspired by CLAPSearch by Dr. Haohe Liu and musicmakerhub by Dr. Tom Collins.
