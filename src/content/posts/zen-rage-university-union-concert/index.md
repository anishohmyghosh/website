This year, with the pressure of the pandemic and the social distancing, I decided to curate my live performance at University Union's Student Concert for self-expression and self-care. I created a contrast between heavy metal and hip-hop performed by a live band. I used digital pitch correction and Ableton Live to bring my voice to life with effects and harmonies. Special thanks to Max Bodner (guitar), Ben Teolis (bass), and Grady Collignbourne (drums) for helping me make this vision a reality!
