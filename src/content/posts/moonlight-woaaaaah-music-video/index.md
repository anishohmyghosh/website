I love this song! I created this music video with the help of Adam Silverberg. The music video follows a character who falls from the moon and lands in New York. The character then experiences the world around him through the internet and hopes to one day return to the moon, where all his dreams come to reality.

I did this project using Premiere Pro, Google Earth, and other astronomical tools. I had a lot of fun overlaying PNG gifs and creating a world through video.

Enjoy!
